About Vector

Delivering Across India

VECTR Private Limited is a newly established logistics and delivery company founded in April 2025. Based in Jaunpur, Uttar Pradesh, we are committed to providing reliable, fast, and affordable Pan India delivery services. Though we are just one month into our journey, our vision is to connect every corner of India with seamless logistics solutions.

Global Alignment

Our Vision & Values

01

Reliability First

We believe every parcel tells a story. From small towns to metro cities, VECTR ensures your shipments reach safely and on time across all Indian states and union territories.

02

Affordable Pricing

As a new company, we understand the value of every rupee. Our competitive pricing structure ensures businesses and individuals can ship without breaking the bank.

03

Pan India Reach

From Kashmir to Kanyakumari, VECTR is building a comprehensive delivery network covering all 28 states and 8 union territories of India with door-to-door service.

Our Story

How It Started

April 2025

Company Founded

VECTR Private Limited was officially registered and incorporated in Jaunpur, Uttar Pradesh, with a vision to provide reliable Pan India delivery services at affordable prices.

May 2025

Operations Launched

Within the first month, VECTR began accepting shipments and delivering parcels across multiple states in India, establishing partnerships with local courier networks.
